## 2. Principles

- We process only the data the platform needs to work.
- No tracking: no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts, no tracking cookies. Fonts

- Statistics about the platform are computed from its own data as sums, shares and medians only;
  they never show single people.
- We do not sell or rent personal data.

## 3. What we process and why

### Your account

- **Data:** email address, username, password (stored only as an Argon2 hash), the time you
  confirmed being at least 16 years old, the confirmation of your email address, two-factor
  methods (authenticator app secret, recovery codes, public keys of passkeys).
- **GitHub:** if you connect GitHub, we store only your GitHub account number, username and
  profile link. Other profile data GitHub sends at sign-in is discarded, and access tokens are not
  stored.
- **Purpose:** to provide your account and keep it secure.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

### Your profile

- **Data:** username and avatar initials are public. Bio, link, interests, operating systems,
  skills and an uploaded avatar are optional, and each is shown only if you switch it on. Profile
  pages are not indexed by search engines unless you allow it.
- **Purpose:** to let others recognise you on the platform.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract; your choice for every optional field.

### Projects, campaigns and participation

- **Data:** projects and test campaigns you create, including descriptions, download links and
  test tasks; invitations; applications with your answers; participations and your task progress
  with optional notes.
- **Purpose:** to bring developers and testers together.
- **Legal basis:** performance of the contract.
- Answers to application questions are deleted when an application is rejected or withdrawn, when
  you leave or are removed, and at the latest 30 days after the campaign ended.

### Feedback

- **Data:** your feedback (texts, environment, version) and attached screenshots or text files.
  Screenshots are re-encoded, which removes embedded metadata such as location.
- **Visibility:** feedback is visible to the project team and the campaign's participants;
  attachments only to the team and you.
- **Publishing:** feedback becomes public only if the team proposes it **and** you agree
  (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). Public feedback never shows your name or attachments, and you can make it
  private again at any time.

### Reports and moderation

- **Reports:** if you report content or a person, moderators see your report and your username;
  the reported team or person does not learn who reported.
- **Moderation records:** warnings, blocks, account suspensions and appeals are stored with their
  reasons so that decisions can be traced.
- **Moderators' access to private content:** moderators can open private content only for a
  reported object, with a stated reason, for 24 hours; every access is recorded.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in a safe platform and the prevention of abuse
  (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

### Security and abuse prevention

- **Rate limits:** to limit abuse, the platform counts requests per account and per IP address.
  IP addresses are stored only as keyed hashes, never in plain text, and are deleted at the latest
  about one day after your last request.
- **Security log:** a log records security events such as sign-ins, password changes, two-factor
  changes, role changes, moderation actions and data exports. It contains no IP addresses.
- **Server logs:** the servers log each request (time, requested address, status code, browser
  identification) and errors; error and sign-in records of the server itself can contain IP
  addresses. The server logs are deleted automatically after 7 days. Only the operator can read
  them; they are used to find faults and attacks.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in the security of the platform.

### Email

- **What we send:** transactional emails only, such as address confirmation, password reset and
  notifications about your campaigns, feedback, reports and moderation decisions. No newsletters.
- **Delivery:** emails are delivered by Scaleway SAS (France) on our behalf.
- **Processing agreement:** Scaleway processes the emails under its data processing agreement,
  which is part of its contract terms.

## 4. Cookies

The platform sets only cookies it needs to work:

- a **session cookie** while you are signed in;
- a **security cookie** (CSRF protection);
- if you choose a light or dark theme, a cookie that **remembers that choice**.

No tracking or advertising cookies are used, so no cookie banner is needed.

## 5. Recipients and processors

Your data is shared only as far as necessary:

- **Infomaniak Network SA (Switzerland):** hosting of the platform and storage of its encrypted
  backups, both in Switzerland.
- **Scaleway SAS (France):** email delivery.
- **GitHub (USA):** only if you sign in with GitHub, connect GitHub, or let us verify a GitHub
  repository. GitHub then processes data under its own privacy policy. For verifying repositories
  on GitLab.com or Codeberg, only the repository address is sent to them.
- **Other users** see what you publish or share with them as described above.

## 6. Transfers abroad

- **Switzerland and the EU:** your data is processed here. The EU offers adequate data protection
  in the view of the Swiss Federal Council.
- **GitHub (USA):** if you use GitHub sign-in, connect GitHub or verify a GitHub repository,
  data is exchanged with GitHub, Inc. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy
  Framework and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For organisations certified under the
  Swiss-U.S. framework, the Swiss Federal Council recognises an adequate level of data protection
  (Data Protection Ordinance, Annex 1); for the EU, the European Commission's adequacy decision
  for the EU-U.S. framework applies. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Your account:** if you delete your account, it becomes invisible at once and is deleted for
  good after 14 days (you can restore it by signing in within that time).
- **Data that remains after deletion:**
  - your feedback remains for the projects, but without any link to you;
  - your reports remain without a link to you;
  - entries in the security log remain without any reference to you.
- **Other periods:** application answers and rate-limit data are kept as stated above.
- **Backups:** the database and uploaded files are copied every night, encrypted, and each copy
  is kept for 7 days. Data deleted on the platform therefore remains in backups for at most
  7 more days; they are used only to restore the platform after a failure.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access:** see your data, including a copy in a machine-readable format;
- **correction and deletion:** correct or delete it;
- **restriction and objection:** restrict or object to its processing;
- **withdrawal:** withdraw your consent, for example by making published feedback private again.

You can download your data yourself under *Settings → Data and account* and delete your account
there. For everything else, write to {{contact_email}}.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and
Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country.

## 9. Security

Measures include encrypted connections, password hashing with Argon2, optional two-factor
authentication and passkeys, a strict separation of private and public content, and regular
security reviews.
