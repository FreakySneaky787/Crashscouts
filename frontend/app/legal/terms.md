## 3. Rules for developers

When you present a project or run a test campaign, you confirm that:

- you have the right to distribute the software, and you do not present someone else's software as
  your own;
- your download links lead to the software you describe and contain no malware, no hidden
  functions and nothing illegal;
- your descriptions are honest, including what the software does with testers' devices and data;
- you treat testers fairly and use their feedback only to improve your software.

Public campaigns are reviewed before they appear. A review is no guarantee that the software is
safe.

## 4. Rules for testers

- **Private campaigns:** keep the content of private campaigns confidential unless the team allows

- **Feedback:** give it in good faith and describe what you actually observed.
- **Respect:** follow the rules of each campaign and the project's licence.

- illegal content or content that violates others' rights;
- attacking, overloading or circumventing the platform's security;
- collecting other users' data.
